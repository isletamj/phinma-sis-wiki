# Registrar Document Monitoring

## Overview

The **Registrar Document Monitoring** page is a dashboard that lets Registrars
monitor, filter, and verify student document submissions across all campuses. It
provides a compliance summary (Compliant, Partially Compliant, Non-Compliant) and
lets Registrars review each student's uploaded documents and record verification
decisions directly in the system.

<Callout type="info">
  This dashboard replaces manual tracking by giving Registrars a centralized view of
  document compliance, with the ability to verify or reject individual documents and
  export filtered lists for reporting.
</Callout>

## How to Access

1. Log in to the PHINMA SIS.
2. Select **Student Details** from the list of modules.
3. Search for **Registrar Document Monitor** using the page search bar.
4. Click the page link to open the dashboard.

## Understanding the Page

The dashboard has a **filter panel** at the top and a **student list** below.

The filter panel requires **Intake**, **Campus**, **College**, **Study Level**, and
**Course**. Click **Show** to load the list, **Export** to download the current
filtered view as CSV, or **Cancel** to reset.

After clicking **Show**, three counters appear:

| Counter | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Compliant Students | All required documents submitted **and** verified by a Registrar. |
| Partially Compliant | Some required documents submitted or verified, but not all. |
| Non-Compliant | No required documents submitted. |

The student list shows Sr. No., **Student ID** (a link that opens the Student Details
popup), **Student Name** (also opens the popup), College, Course, and Semester Name.

<Callout type="info">
  The counters reflect the filtered population. Changing a filter and clicking
  **Show** again refreshes the counts.
</Callout>

## Verifying Student Documents

<Steps>

#### Filter and load students

Select the **Intake**, **Campus**, **College**, **Study Level**, and **Course**, then
click **Show**.

#### Open student details

Click a student's row or Student ID. A **Student Details** popup opens with their ID
and name, course and semester, and a document list.

#### Review the document list

The document table shows each mapped document with its **Mandatory** flag,
**Uploaded** flag, a **View** (eye) icon to preview the file, **Status** (Pending /
Verified / Rejected), **Verified By**, **Verified Date**, and **Remarks**.

<Callout type="important">
  An uploaded document is **not** automatically verified. Registrar verification is a
  separate action, and original documents must be submitted to the Registrar for
  verification.
</Callout>

#### Record your decision

Check one or more documents, choose **Verified**, **Rejected**, or **Pending** in
the **Status** dropdown, add notes in **Remarks** (max 100 characters), and click
**Submit**. The Status, Verified By, and Verified Date columns update.

#### Close or continue

Click **Close** to return to the student list, and repeat for other students.

</Steps>

## Exporting the Filtered List

After filtering and clicking **Show**, click **Export** to download the student list
as CSV. The export honors the active filters and includes per-document submission and
verification status.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**A student uploaded all their documents but still shows as Partially Compliant. Is this a bug?**
No — uploading and verification are separate steps. A student is fully **Compliant**
only once their required documents are both uploaded and verified by a Registrar.
Complete the verification for any documents still Pending.

**Can I verify multiple documents for the same student at once?**
Yes. In the popup, select multiple documents, choose a Status, add Remarks, and click
**Submit** — the same status and remarks apply to all selected documents.

**Do the counters count documents or students?**
They reflect compliance at the **student** level, but each student's status is
computed from whether their individual required documents are submitted and verified.

**My exported CSV is missing columns I expected. What should I include in a support ticket?**
The filters you applied, a screenshot of the export, the expected columns, and your
campus and role.
