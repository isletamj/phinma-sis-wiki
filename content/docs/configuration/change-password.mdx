# Change Password

## Overview

The **Change Password** page lets a logged-in SIS user reset their own account
password. You supply your current password, set a new one, solve a math captcha,
and verify the change with a one-time password (OTP) sent to your registered email.
The page enforces a password-strength policy and validates the OTP, so the change
can't complete without a correct, single-use code.

<Callout type="warning">
  Changing your password here signs you out of **every other browser and device**
  where your account is signed in. You'll need to log in again on those devices
  with the new password.
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

- You know your **current password**. If you've forgotten it, use the **Forgot
  Password** flow on the login screen instead.
- Your **registered email** in the SIS is active and accessible — the OTP is sent
  there.

## How to Access

The Change Password page isn't a module tile — it lives in the user avatar dropdown
at the top-right of every SIS screen.

1. Log in to the PHINMA SIS portal.
2. Click your **avatar** (your first-name initial) at the top-right.
3. In the dropdown, click **Change Password**.

<Callout type="info">
  If you see an **Expires in N Days** badge under the Change Password option, your
  current password is nearing expiry. Change it before the badge runs out, or
  you'll be forced to change it at your next login.
</Callout>

## Changing Your Password

<Steps>

#### Enter your old and new passwords

Type your current password into **Old Password**, then your new one into **New
Password** and again into **Confirm Password**. Watch the strength indicator below
the form and keep adjusting until it reads at least **Strong**.

#### Confirm your contact details and solve the captcha

**Mobile No.** and **Email** are pre-filled from your account. Check the email is
correct — the OTP goes there. Then solve the math captcha (e.g. `77 + 5 = __`) and
type the answer in the box.

<Callout type="warning">
  If the email shown is wrong, do **not** continue. Cancel and contact SIS Support
  to update your registered email first — otherwise the OTP goes to an inbox you
  can't reach.
</Callout>

#### Request the OTP

Click **Get OTP**. A popup confirms the code was sent — click **OK**. The form then
shows an **Enter OTP** field and the buttons change to **Resend OTP · Submit ·
Cancel**.

#### Enter the OTP and submit

Open the email from PHINMA SIS, copy the OTP (alphanumeric and **case-sensitive**,
e.g. `0sLz9R`), paste it into **Enter OTP**, solve the new captcha, and click
**Submit**.

<Callout type="info">
  If the OTP doesn't work, click **Resend OTP** for a fresh code. The previous OTP
  becomes invalid the moment a new one is requested.
</Callout>

#### Confirm success and sign in again

On success, a popup reads "Your Password successfully reset". Click **OK**. All your
other active SIS sessions are signed out automatically — sign in again with your new
password.

</Steps>

## Common Errors

**"Entered OTP is Invalid."**
The OTP doesn't match the most recent code — usually because it expired, was
mistyped, or a newer OTP was generated. Use the **most recent** OTP in your email,
check character by character (OTPs are case-sensitive), and click **Resend OTP** to
issue a fresh code if needed.

**Strength indicator stays at "Weak" or "Medium".**
The password doesn't meet the policy. Click **Password Policy** (top-right of the
New Password field) for the full rules. Avoid disallowed special characters and
combinations, and aim for length plus a mix of upper/lower case, digits, and an
allowed symbol such as `@` or `.`.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where does the OTP get sent?**
To the **Email** address shown on the form, pulled from your account profile. If
it's wrong, cancel and contact SIS Support to update it first.

**How long is the OTP valid?**
It's time-bound and single-use. If it stops working, click **Resend OTP** — a new
code immediately invalidates the previous one.

**Will I be signed out of other devices?**
Yes. Changing your password invalidates every other active session on your account.
Sign in again on each device with the new password.

**Can I reuse my old password as the new one?**
No. The new password must differ from the current one.

**I forgot my current password. Can I still use this page?**
No. The **Old Password** field is required and verified. Use the **Forgot
Password** link on the login screen instead.
