# SWU Retention Policy

## Overview

The **SWU Retention Policy** pages are used to configure SWU's revised Academic
Eligibility Policy for tertiary programs and to notify students who fall under
it. Once a policy is configured, the system evaluates each student's failed
subjects at enlistment and applies one of two outcomes automatically:
**deloading** (a warning or probation, plus a forced drop of one or two subjects)
or **disqualification** (the student is blocked from enlisting and advised to
shift to another program).

<Callout type="info">
  This page covers **SWU's** Academic Eligibility (Retention) Policy, which runs
  on its own configuration and reminder pages. All other PHINMA Education Network
  (PEN) schools use the standard
  [Retention Policy](/docs/academics/retention-policy) page, which has a different
  rule set.
</Callout>

<Callout type="important">
  The configuration page stores the **policy scope and exclusions** only.
  Enforcement happens downstream at **Enlistment By Student**, and the remediation
  path for disqualified students is the **Course Transfer** page.
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

- The **Intake**, **Campus**, **College**, **Study Level**, and **Course** are
  already configured in the system.
- **Grade encoding for previous semesters is complete.** Every rule counts failed
  subjects from grade history — missing or unposted grades will silently
  under-count a student's failures.
- For transferees and shiftees, **prior-school or prior-program grades have been
  migrated and credited**. Failures that were never migrated cannot be counted.
- Student emails are populated if you plan to use the reminders page.

## How to Access

The policy uses two separate pages under the **Academics** module.

**Retention Configuration** — where the policy itself is set up.

1. Log in to the PHINMA SIS portal as **Admin**.
2. From the list of modules, click **Academics**.
3. Open **Enlistment Management → Retention Configuration**.

**Retention Reminders** — a separate page, where affected students are notified.

1. From the list of modules, click **Academics**.
2. Open **Enlistment Management → Retention Reminders**.

<Callout type="info">
  Both pages are commonly referred to in shorthand as **Academics → Retention
  Configuration** and **Academics → Retention Reminders**.
</Callout>

## Understanding the Page

The configuration page is a form at the top with a saved-records grid at the
bottom.

Form fields:

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Intake** | Yes | Admission batch the policy applies to (e.g., 2024-2025). The policy is scoped by intake, not by academic session. |
| **Campus** | Yes | Campus where the policy is enforced. |

## How the Rules Work

Every rule counts **failed subjects** from the student's grade history within the
configured scope. There are two tiers of consequence — deloading and
disqualification — plus a write-off clause that can reverse a count.

<Callout type="important">
  "Deloading" means the system **drops** subjects from the student's next-semester
  enlistment. Drops are taken from the **regular panel subjects only**.
</Callout>

### Deloading Rules

| Trigger | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Failed **2 different subjects** | **Written warning** (sent by email) + **1 subject dropped** next semester |
| Failed **3 different subjects** | **Probation** + **2 subjects dropped** next semester |
| Failed the **same subject twice** | **Probation** + **2 subjects dropped** next semester |

### Disqualification Rules

| Trigger | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Failed **4 different subjects** | Advised to **shift to another program** (if the subjects are not critical) |
| Failed the **same subject 3 times** (3-strike) | Advised to **shift to another program** (if the subjects are not critical) |

A disqualified student cannot complete enlistment — the **Submit and Next** button
is removed — and must be routed through the **Course Transfer** page.

### Write-off Clause

If a student **passes a failed subject on the 1st or 2nd retake**, that subject is
**removed from the disqualification count**. A student who recovers a subject
early therefore does not carry it forward toward the 4-subject or 3-strike
thresholds.

### Excluded Subjects

The following subject classifications have **no impact** on either deloading or
disqualification:

- Physical Education
- SSP
- NSTP
- Research
- Clinics (Dentistry, Optometry, Veterinary Medicine)
- MTAP and Special Topics for MT

### No Credit (NC) Policy

| Student | Does an NC count? |
| --- | --- |
| **First-year student** | **No** — the student may simply retake the subject. NC does not count toward deloading or disqualification. |
| **Upper-year student** | **Yes** — NC is counted, **even if the subject is a first-year subject**. |

## Special Admission Types

### Shiftees and Transferee-Shiftees

If the failed subjects are **critical / board subjects**, the general policy
applies and the student is placed on **probationary status**.

### Transferees

Transferees must meet the **residency requirement — 2 years or 4 semesters**,
including OJT/internship.

| Prior-school record (same program) | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| **2 failures** | Accepted **on probation** + **2 subjects deloaded** |
| **3 or more failures** | **Not accepted** — advised to enroll in another program |

<Callout type="info">
  Special rules apply for **Optometry, Dentistry, and Veterinary Medicine** — check
  with the Dean before processing these programs.
</Callout>

### Returnees

Returnees must follow the **latest curriculum** in effect at the time of return.

- The **Dean evaluates** the old curriculum for creditable subjects.
- The student must complete the latest curriculum's requirements.
- Refer to **Academics Memo 2007-06**.

## Configuring the SWU Retention Policy

<Steps>

#### Open Retention Configuration

Go to **Academics → Retention Configuration**.

#### Set the academic scope

Select **Intake**, **Campus**, **College**, **Study Level**, and **Course**, then
set the **Starting Study Level** — the year level from which the policy begins to
apply.

#### Set the excluded subjects

Select the subject classifications that should be exempt from the fail count. If
you leave this blank, all subjects in the student's grade history will be counted.

<Callout type="warning">
  Excluded subjects are the most common source of wrong outcomes. If PE, NSTP,
  SSP, Research, Clinics, or MTAP/Special Topics are not excluded on the
  configuration, students will be deloaded or disqualified on subjects that policy
  says should never count.
</Callout>

#### Submit

Click **Submit**. The saved configuration appears in the grid below the form.

<Callout type="warning">
  **After submitting:** If a configuration already exists for the same academic
  scope, the system rejects the save with a *record already exists* message.
  Locate the existing row rather than creating a duplicate.
</Callout>

</Steps>

## Sending Retention Reminders

1. Open the **Academics → Retention Reminders** page as **Admin**.
2. Filter by the applicable academic scope to populate the at-risk list.
3. Tick the students you want to notify and click **Send Reminder**.
4. Refresh and confirm the send is recorded — **Email Status**, **Sent By**, and
   **Sent On** are stamped on the row.

The advisory email tells the student they are not eligible to enroll in the next
semester of the same course, names their curriculum and the affected subjects and
subject codes, and directs them to coordinate with the Registrar for a transfer.

<Callout type="info">
  The reminder template is currently **fixed** — the wording cannot be edited per
  school or per case. A separate ticket has been raised to make the template
  customisable.
</Callout>

## Generating the Retention Policy Report

To get the at-risk list as an export for the Registrar's caseload:

1. Open the reports page and select the report type **Retention Policy Report
   SWU**.
2. Select the **Intake**, **Campus**, **College**, and optionally the **Course** —
   the report is filtered by intake, not by academic session.
3. Generate and download the report.

The export lists the affected students with their rule, subject, and status
details, and covers deloading, disqualification, and
transferee/shiftee/returnee cases.

## Downstream Behavior at Enlistment

Enforcement happens on **Student Details → Enlistment By Student**.

**If the student falls under a deloading rule:** a note appears indicating the
student is under the deloading rules, and the system prompts for the required
subject drops — one subject for a written warning, two for probation. Drops are
taken from the regular panel subjects only.

**If the student falls under a disqualification rule:** an alert advises a shift
to another program, and the **Submit and Next** button is removed so enlistment
cannot be completed. The student must go to the Registrar, who processes a
transfer on the **Course Transfer** page with a clear remark such as *"due to
retention policy."*

<Callout type="warning">
  Course transfers cannot be undone once completed. Verify the target program and
  curriculum before confirming.
</Callout>

## Common Errors

**Required fields are not selected.**
Submit was clicked before the academic scope was fully selected. Complete every
required dropdown — Intake, Campus, College, Study Level, Course, and Starting
Study Level — then submit again.

**A duplicate configuration is rejected.**
A configuration already exists for the same academic scope. Cancel the form, find
the existing row in the grid, and work from it instead of creating a second row.

**A student was deloaded on a subject that should not count.**
The subject's classification was not added to **Excluded Subjects** on the
configuration. Check the configuration row for the student's scope, confirm PE /
SSP / NSTP / Research / Clinics / MTAP-Special Topics are all excluded, and
correct the row. Then re-check the student at enlistment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why doesn't this look like the Retention Policy page the other schools use?**
Because it isn't. SWU's Academic Eligibility Policy is a different policy with a
different rule set — deloading and disqualification instead of Three-Strike /
Fail-Twice / GRR — so it was built as a separate page with its own configuration,
reminders, and report.

**Does a failed PE or NSTP subject count against a student?**
No, provided those classifications were added to **Excluded Subjects** on the
configuration. The exclusions are applied from the configuration row, not
hard-coded, so a missing exclusion will cause the subject to be counted.

**The student failed the same subject twice. Is that deloading or disqualification?**
Deloading. Failing the same subject **twice** puts the student on probation with
two subjects dropped. It becomes disqualification at the **third** failure of the
same subject.

**A student passed a failed subject on retake — do they still carry the failure?**
Not for disqualification. Under the Write-off Clause, passing on the 1st or 2nd
retake removes that subject from the disqualification count.

**Do failures from a previous school or a previous program count?**
Yes, provided the records have been migrated and credited. Transferee and shiftee
failures count toward the retention triggers once they exist in the system — if
the grades were never migrated, the system cannot see them.

**Can I edit the reminder email wording?**
Not currently. The template is fixed; a separate ticket has been raised to make it
customisable.
