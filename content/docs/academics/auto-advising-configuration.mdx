# Auto Advising Configuration

## Overview

The **Auto Advising Configuration** page decides which curricula and semesters are
allowed to skip advisor approval during enlistment. It allows registrars and
academic administrators to switch **auto-advising** on or off for a chosen set of
curricula and year-semester codes within one academic session, campus and college.

When auto-advising is **on** for a combination, a **regular** student's enlistment is
treated as final the moment they submit it — no advisor approval step. When it is
**off**, that student's enlistment is held and routed to the advisor for approval
before it can proceed.

<Callout type="important">
  Auto-advising only ever applies to **regular** students. An **irregular**
  student's enlistment is always sent to the advisor for approval, no matter what is
  configured here. See *What Auto-Advising Changes for Students* below.
</Callout>

## What Auto-Advising Changes for Students

This page has no effect of its own — it changes what happens on the **enlistment**
side, at the moment a student submits their enlistment for the session.

| | Auto-advising **on** | Auto-advising **off** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Regular student submits an enlistment** | The enlistment is treated as **final** on submission. No advisor is involved, and the student moves straight on to the next step. | The enlistment is **held** and sent to the student's advisor in **Enlistment Approval**. |
| **What the advisor can do** | Nothing to do — the enlistment never reaches them. | Review it, **change the subjects** on it, add a **remark**, then approve it. |
| **When the ORF is generated** | After the student submits. | Only after the advisor approves. |
| **What the student sees meanwhile** | The enlistment proceeds. | *Pending for Approval*, plus any remark the advisor added. |
| **Irregular student submits an enlistment** | **No change** — still sent to the advisor for approval. | Sent to the advisor for approval. |

Three things follow from this that are worth being clear about:

**It never applies to irregular students.** Auto-advising is a shortcut for the
straightforward case only. An irregular student **always** goes through Enlistment
Approval, and this page cannot change that. A student is irregular when **any one**
of the following is true:

- They have a **failing grade in a prerequisite subject**.
- **Subject sequencing has not been followed.**
- They are **re-studying a subject** — that is, carrying back subjects.
- They enlisted **less than the maximum load** for the term.

<Callout type="warning">
  The asymmetry that catches people out: enlisting a **full load** only clears the
  last reason on that list. A student carrying re-study or back subjects is
  irregular whatever their load, and their enlistment still waits for the advisor. A
  full load does not make an irregular student regular.
</Callout>

The first three reasons come from the student's own academic record; the fourth
comes from the enlistment they have just submitted. You configure this page for the
curriculum and semester — the system then decides student by student who actually
qualifies.

**The scope is narrower than "a course".** A row switches auto-advising on for one
**curriculum year** and one **semester code**, inside one academic session, campus
and college. Students on the same program but a different curriculum year, or in a
semester code you did not tick, are unaffected. This is the most common reason a
student who "should" be auto-advised is not.

**It is read at submission, not applied retroactively.** Switching a row on part-way
through an enlistment period does nothing for enlistments already sitting with an
advisor — those still need the advisor to act. Switching a row off does not pull
back enlistments that already went through.

## Prerequisites

Before using this page, make sure the following are in place:

- The **academic session** you are configuring exists and is active.
- The **curriculum** exists and is **active** for the selected campus and college —
  only active curricula are listed here.
- The **year-and-semester codes** (Y1S1, Y2S2, and so on) you intend to select are
  defined in that curriculum.
- Enlistment for the session has **not yet opened**, or you accept that the change
  only affects enlistments submitted after it is saved.
- You have access to **Enlistment Management → Auto Advising Configuration**.

## How to Access

1. Log in to the PHINMA SIS portal.
2. From the top navigation, click **Enlistment Management**.
3. From the dropdown, select **Auto Advising Configuration**.

## Understanding the Page

The page has two parts stacked down the screen:

1. A **form** at the top, where you pick what to configure and whether it is active.
2. The **Auto Advisory List** underneath, showing the configurations that already
   exist for the session, campus and college you have selected. This panel is
   **hidden entirely** until you have selected all three, and stays hidden if
   nothing has been configured for that combination.

The first three fields are **cascading**: each one stays empty until the one before
it has a value.

**Academic Session → Campus → College → (Curriculum + Semester)**

**Form fields:**

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Academic Session** | Yes | The term the setting applies to. Listed newest first (for example *Summer 2026*, then *SY 26-27 SEM I*, then *SY 25-26 SEM II*). Setting this is what populates Campus. Auto-advising is configured **per session** — it does not carry over from one term to the next. |
| **Campus** | Yes | The campuses available to your account, populated from the selected session. |
| **College** | Yes | Populated from the selected Campus. |
| **Curriculum** | Yes | **Multi-select.** Entries read *College abbreviation - Program name - curriculum year*, e.g. *CCJE - Bachelor of Science in Criminology - 23-24*. Only **active** curricula appear. The list has its own **Search** box and a **Select all** option. |
| **Semester** | Yes | **Multi-select.** The curriculum's own year-and-semester codes — **Y1S1**, **Y1S2**, **Y2S1**, the trimester codes **Y1T1**–**Y3T3**, and the summer codes such as **Y2 Summer**. This is *not* the academic session. Also has **Search** and **Select all**. |
| **Status** | Yes | A toggle. Switched **on** (blue) means auto-advising is **active** for the combinations you selected; switched **off** (grey) means **inactive**. It starts **on** by default on a blank form. |

**Auto Advisory List columns:**

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Action** | An edit icon (✏️). Opens that record in the form above so its status can be changed — see *Activating or Deactivating an Existing Configuration*. |
| **Academic Session** | The session the record was saved against. |
| **Campus** | The campus the record covers. |
| **College** | The college the record covers. |
| **Curriculum** | The single curriculum, including its curriculum year, that the record covers. |
| **Semester** | The single year-and-semester code the record covers. |
| **Status** | **Active** (green badge) or inactive. |

The list has the standard SIS table controls: **Show *N* entries** (10, 25, 50 or
100), a **Search** box that filters the rows on screen, sortable column headers, an
**Export** button, and pagination beneath.

<Callout type="warning">
  The Status label does not change. The text beside the toggle reads **Is Active**
  whether the switch is on or off. Read the **switch position and colour**, not the
  wording, to tell which state you are about to save.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Curriculum and Semester are combined as a **cross-product**, and each combination
  is stored as its own row in the Auto Advisory List. Selecting 3 curricula and 2
  semesters creates **6** rows in one submission. Select only the semesters that
  genuinely apply to every curriculum in your selection, or split the work into more
  than one submission.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  The list is filtered by the **Academic Session, Campus and College** selected
  above it — it is not a view of everything configured across the school. Change the
  college and you are looking at a different list. Curriculum and Semester do not
  filter it.
</Callout>

## Turning Auto-Advising On for a Curriculum and Semester

<Steps>

#### Identify the session, campus and college

Work down the first three fields in order: **Academic Session**, then **Campus**,
then **College**. Each selection reloads the page and fills the dropdown below it,
so give each one a moment before moving on.

Choose the academic session the enlistment will actually happen in. A setting saved
against *SY 25-26 SEM II* does nothing for *SY 26-27 SEM I*.

Once all three are set, check the **Auto Advisory List** at the bottom of the page
before adding anything — the combination you are about to configure may already be
there.

#### Select the curricula

Open **Curriculum** and tick every curriculum this setting should apply to. Use the
**Search** box to narrow a long list, or **Select all** to take the whole college at
once.

Pick the **curriculum year** the affected students actually follow — a college
usually has several years of the same program listed (for example *- 18-19*, *-
22-23*, *- 23-24*), and each one is configured separately.

#### Select the semesters

Open **Semester** and tick the year-and-semester codes the setting applies to. These
are the **curriculum's own** codes (Y1S1, Y2S2, Y1T1, Y2 Summer), not the academic
session.

Remember the cross-product: every curriculum you ticked in the previous step is
paired with every semester you tick here, and each pair becomes its own row.

#### Set the status

Leave the **Status** toggle **on** to activate auto-advising for the selected
combinations.

#### Submit

Click **Submit** to save. Nothing is applied until you do. The new rows then appear
in the **Auto Advisory List** below.

**Cancel** clears the whole form back to *Please Select* and hides the Auto Advisory
List. It does not undo anything already saved.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Set this before enlistment opens. The configuration is read when a student submits
  their enlistment. Switching it on midway through an enlistment period does not
  retroactively approve enlistments that have already been routed to an advisor —
  those still need the advisor to act on them.
</Callout>

## Reviewing What Is Already Configured

To check whether auto-advising is on for a particular program:

1. Set **Academic Session**, **Campus** and **College**.
2. Read the **Auto Advisory List** that appears below the form.
3. Use the list's own **Search** box to jump to a curriculum or semester code, or
   raise **Show *N* entries** to 25, 50 or 100 to see more rows at once.
4. Use **Export** if you want the list outside the system.

If no list appears at all, nothing has been configured for that session, campus and
college.

<Callout type="info">
  Leave **Curriculum** and **Semester** empty while you are only reading. They do
  not filter the list, and filling them in is the first step towards creating new
  rows.
</Callout>

## Activating or Deactivating an Existing Configuration

A row that already exists is changed from the list, not by re-entering it in the
form.

1. Set **Academic Session**, **Campus** and **College** so the row you want appears
   in the **Auto Advisory List**.
2. Click the **edit icon (✏️)** in that row's **Action** column.
3. The form above loads with that record's values, and every selection field —
   Academic Session, Campus, College, Curriculum and Semester — becomes **locked**.
   Only the **Status** toggle can be changed.
4. Switch **Status** on or off as required.
5. Click **Submit** to save the change, or **Cancel** to leave the record as it is.

<Callout type="important">
  Editing an existing record can only turn it on or off. The curriculum and semester
  it applies to cannot be changed here — if a row was created against the wrong
  curriculum year or semester code, deactivate it and create the correct one.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  Switching a row off puts that curriculum and semester back under advisor approval
  for every student in it, regular students included.
</Callout>

## Key Scenarios

**Scenario 1 — Opening a term for regular students**
Enlistment for the new semester is about to open and the registrar wants regular
students to enlist without waiting on an advisor. They select the academic session,
campus and college, tick all of the college's active curricula and the semester
codes in play for the term, leave the Status toggle on, and submit. The new rows
appear in the Auto Advisory List, and regular students who enlist afterwards see
their enlistment go straight through; irregular students still land with their
advisor.

**Scenario 2 — Holding one program back for review**
A college wants its graduating-year students screened by an advisor before
enlistment is final, even the regular ones. The administrator configures the
college's curricula with the earlier semester codes only, leaving the graduating-year
code out — so those students continue to route through advisor approval while
everyone else is auto-advised.

**Scenario 3 — A student who is still waiting for approval**
A student reports that their enlistment shows *Pending for Approval* even though
auto-advising was set up. The administrator loads the session, campus and college
and checks the Auto Advisory List for a row matching the student's exact curriculum
year and semester code, with Status **Active**. If the row is there and active, the
next thing to check is whether the student is actually regular — back subjects, a
broken subject sequence, or an under-loaded enlistment each make them irregular, and
advisor approval is expected in that case.

## Common Errors

**The Campus or College dropdown is empty**
The field above it has not been set, or your account has no campus or college mapped
to it for that session. Set **Academic Session** first, then **Campus**, then
**College** — each one populates the next — and give the page a moment after each
selection, since it reloads between steps. If Campus stays empty on a session you
know is open, raise a support ticket; your user account may not be mapped to a
campus.

**The Auto Advisory List does not appear**
Either the session, campus and college are not all selected yet, or nothing has been
configured for that combination. Complete all three of **Academic Session**,
**Campus** and **College**. If the panel still does not appear, there are no records
for that combination — check the other campuses and colleges the program might have
been configured under before assuming the configuration is missing.

**The curriculum I need is not in the Curriculum list**
Only **active** curricula for the selected campus and college are listed. Confirm
the **Campus** and **College** above match where the curriculum actually sits, check
that the curriculum is still marked active in the curriculum setup for that college,
and check the **curriculum year** — the program may be listed under a different year
than the one you expected.

**The semester code I need is not in the Semester list**
The Semester list carries the year-and-semester codes defined in the curricula
available to the selected college. A code that is not defined does not appear.
Confirm you are looking for the **curriculum's** code (Y1S1, Y2S2, Y1T1) and not the
academic session name. If a code is genuinely missing from a curriculum, it has to be
added to the curriculum before it can be configured here.

**A regular student is still being sent to the advisor**
Either the combination the student sits in has no active row, or the student is not
regular.

- Load the **Auto Advisory List** for the student's academic session, campus and
  college and confirm a row exists for their exact **curriculum year** and
  **semester code**, with Status **Active**.
- Check whether the student is irregular. A failing grade in a prerequisite, subject
  sequencing not followed, a subject being re-studied, or **an enlistment below the
  maximum load** each send the enlistment to the advisor regardless of this page.
  Note that a full load only clears the last of those — a student with back subjects
  stays irregular whatever they enlist.
- Remember that enlistments already routed to an advisor before the row was
  activated stay with the advisor.
- If none of the above explains it, raise a support ticket with the student number,
  the academic session, and the curriculum and semester the student is under.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does auto-advising actually do?**
It removes the advisor approval step for regular students. With it on, the
enlistment a student submits is final and the ORF proceeds; with it off, the
enlistment sits with the advisor until they approve it.

**Does this page override advisor approval for irregular students?**
No. Irregular students always go to the advisor. This page only affects students the
system considers regular for that term.

**My student is irregular but enlisted a full load. Shouldn't they be auto-advised?**
No. Enlisting a full load only removes *one* of the reasons a student can be
irregular — under-loading. If they are also carrying re-study or back subjects, or
their subject sequencing is broken, they stay irregular and the enlistment still
goes to the advisor.

**A regular student enlisted fewer subjects than the full load. Why did their enlistment go to the advisor?**
Because an under-loaded enlistment is itself a reason to be treated as irregular. A
student who takes less than the maximum load for the term is sent for enlistment
advising even if nothing else about their record is unusual.

**Do I have to set this up again every semester?**
Yes. Each row is tied to an academic session, so each new term needs its own
configuration.

**Can I configure more than one curriculum at a time?**
Yes. Both **Curriculum** and **Semester** are multi-selects with a **Select all**
option, and one submission creates a row for every curriculum-and-semester pair you
tick.

**Where can I see what is already configured?**
In the **Auto Advisory List** at the bottom of the page. Set the academic session,
campus and college and the list appears; it shows only the records for that
combination.

**Can I change the curriculum or semester on a record I already saved?**
No. Opening a record with the edit icon locks every field except **Status**. To
correct a wrong curriculum year or semester code, deactivate that row and create the
right one.

**The toggle still says "Is Active" after I switched it off. Did it not work?**
The label is fixed text and does not change. Judge the state from the switch itself:
blue and to the right is active, grey and to the left is inactive. The **Status**
column in the Auto Advisory List is the reliable confirmation of what was actually
saved.

**I switched auto-advising on after students had already enlisted. Why are their enlistments still pending?**
The setting is read at the moment a student submits. Enlistments already routed to
an advisor stay there and need the advisor to act on them.

**Can I set a minimum GWA so only students above it are auto-advised?**
Not on this page. The only control here is on or off for a curriculum and semester.
