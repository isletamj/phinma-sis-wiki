---
title: Batch enrollment processing
date: 2026-07-15
---

Registrars can now process an entire section's enrollment in one pass instead
of student by student. Upload a CSV, review the parsed rows, then commit.

<Section title="New">
- Bulk enrollment import via CSV, with a preview step before committing
- Per-row validation errors that link straight to the offending student record
</Section>

<Section title="Improvements">
- Section rosters load noticeably faster on large campuses
- Clearer wording on the enrollment confirmation dialog
</Section>

<Section title="Bug fixes">
- Fixed subjects with no assigned instructor being dropped from the roster export
</Section>
